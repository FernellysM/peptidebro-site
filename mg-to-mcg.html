How to convert mg to mcg

mcg = mg × 1,000
mg = mcg ÷ 1,000

A milligram is one thousandth of a gram; a microgram is one millionth. That makes 1 mg equal to 1,000 mcg. The symbol µg also means micrograms. Source: NIST: Metric prefixes.

Can mg be converted directly to mL?

No. Milligrams measure mass and millilitres measure volume. You need the solution concentration to connect the two. Use the concentration calculator below to calculate mass per millilitre.
