Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Cuff ("the app") is a blood pressure logging and lifestyle journal for general wellness. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers. We do not operate servers that receive your data. There is no account, no sign-up, and no email required.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter into Cuff, including blood pressure readings, pulse, notes, daily check-ins (sodium, alcohol, caffeine, exercise, sleep, stress), and medication reminders, is stored on your device in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app removes this on-device copy.

Apple Health (optional)

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Cuff can save the blood pressure and pulse readings you enter to Apple Health, and read blood pressure readings written by other apps or devices so they appear in your log. Health data stays in Apple's encrypted Health store on your device and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. We never receive, see, or store your Health data. Health data is never used for advertising or shared with third parties. You can revoke Health access at any time in iOS Settings.

Third parties

The app does not include third-party analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Cuff Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple may process purchase and subscription information under Apple's own privacy policy. Cuff does not receive, store, or sell your Apple ID, payment details, or App Store account information.

Network

The app does not send your readings, check-ins, or settings to us. Network activity is limited to Apple services: loading subscription products, verifying App Store purchase entitlement, and links you choose to open (Privacy, Terms, Manage Subscriptions). Those destinations are handled by Apple or your browser.

Reports and exports

PDF reports and CSV exports are generated on your device. They are shared only when and where you choose to share them, using the standard iOS share sheet.

Notifications

If you enable reading or medication reminders, Cuff schedules local notifications on your device. Reminder settings are not sent to us. You can manage notification permission in iOS Settings.

Not a medical device

Cuff does not measure blood pressure, does not diagnose or treat any condition, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Reference ranges shown in the app are general educational information published by recognized health organizations. Always consult a qualified clinician about your readings.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children.

Changes

If we ever change this policy, the "Last updated" date above will change. Substantive changes will be surfaced in-app.
