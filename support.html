Support

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Need help with PeptideBro, have a billing question, or want to report a bug? Email us and include the app version, your device model, and a short description of what happened.

Email: support@peptidebro.app

Subscription help

PeptideBro Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. You can manage, cancel, or restore subscriptions from iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Privacy and local data

PeptideBro stores journal entries, stack items, notes, and reminder settings on your device. We cannot recover local app data if the app is deleted or the device is erased.

Medical questions

PeptideBro is an educational tracking journal, not medical advice. For questions about a protocol, dose, side effect, or health decision, contact a licensed healthcare professional.
