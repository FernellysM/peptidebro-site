U-100 scale conversion

U-100 markings = mL × 100
mL = U-100 markings ÷ 100

A U-100 syringe scale corresponds to 100 markings per millilitre. For example, 0.1 mL corresponds to the 10-unit mark. These are scale conversions: they do not tell you the mass or biological activity of a substance. Reference: BD medication delivery catalogue.

Why specify U-100?

Different syringe scales use different conversions. This page supports U-100 only; do not apply its result to another scale. Check the syringe label, capacity, and smallest marked increment. A mathematical result can fall between markings or exceed a syringe's capacity.

Are syringe units the same as mg?

No. A syringe marking describes volume on a particular scale. Milligrams describe mass. Connecting the two requires the solution's concentration; this converter does not infer it or recommend an amount to administer.
