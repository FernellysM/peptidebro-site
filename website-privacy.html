Optional website analytics

Updated September 11, 2026. This notice supplements our Privacy Policy for peptidebro.app. The iOS app is unchanged.

If you choose Allow analytics, we load Google Analytics to measure page visits, referring website domains, and clicks to the App Store. Google processes technical information such as browser and device information and your network address, and uses analytics cookies to distinguish visits. Our analytics cookies expire after 90 days. Google Analytics provides aggregate traffic reports.

We never read or send calculator inputs, doses, compound names, journals, or shared calculation contents to analytics. Analytics does not run on transfer pages or URLs with query strings or fragments. Automatic form tracking, Google signals, advertising personalization and optional account data sharing are disabled.

Before your permission, we do not load Google Analytics or send analytics requests. Choose No thanks to use the site without analytics. Change your choice with Analytics preferences below; withdrawing consent stops future collection and clears this site's Google Analytics cookies. Your choice is stored in your browser. We honor Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track by disabling analytics.

Google Search Console separately provides aggregate reports about the site's presence in Google Search; it does not add a tracking script to the calculator.
